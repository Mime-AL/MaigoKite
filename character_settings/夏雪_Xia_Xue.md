# 夏雪 (Xia Xue) - Character Profile

## Basic Information
- **Name:** 夏雪 / Xia Xue
- **Age:** 17 years old
- **Grade:** Grade 11 (same class as protagonist, different group)
- **Height:** 162cm
- **Birthday:** June 21st (Summer Solstice)
- **Zodiac:** Cancer

## Personality Traits
- **Surface:** Cheerful, energetic, popular among classmates
- **Inner Nature:** Sensitive about appearance, secretly dreams of being a musician
- **Strengths:** Excellent at socializing, optimistic, creative
- **Weaknesses:** Insecure about singing voice, easily influenced by others' opinions

## Appearance
- **Hair:** Black with subtle pink highlights (gradually fades)
- **Eyes:** Warm brown, slightly downcast when nervous
- **Style:** Colorful casual wear, often wears accessories (earrings, hair clips)
- **Signature Item:** A small guitar-shaped keychain

## Exclusive Events

### Event 1: Rooftop Concert Practice (Affection 35+)
- **Location:** School rooftop at sunset
- **Trigger:** She asks for help with her music practice
- **Branch A (Success):** You encourage her despite imperfections → +20 Affection, unlocks "Private Music Room" activity
- **Branch B (Failure):** She gets discouraged about her voice → +5 Affection, triggers consolation event

### Event 2: Summer Festival Performance (Affection 55+)
- **Location:** School summer festival stage
- **Trigger:** She wants to perform but is afraid of failure
- **Branch A (Success):** You prepare backup plan and support her during performance → +30 Affection, major milestone event
- **Branch B (Failure):** Performance goes poorly without support → -15 Affection, requires recovery activities

### Event 3: Secret Song Exchange (Affection 75+)
- **Location:** Private music room or quiet corner
- **Trigger:** She composes a song for you
- **Outcome:** Exclusive romantic moment, leads to "Deep Bond" stage
- **Special Dialogue:** Reveals her deepest feelings about wanting to be with you

### Event 4: Graduation Music Concert (Affection 85+)
- **Location:** School auditorium
- **Trigger:** Final school event
- **Outcome:** If in romantic relationship → joint solo performance; if not → heartfelt farewell message

Xia Xue has a hidden "Confidence" stat that affects event outcomes:
- Each positive interaction increases confidence
- Negative events or criticism decrease it
- If confidence drops below 20%, she may temporarily reject affection gains
- Recovery activities: Compliments (+5), shared victories (+10)
